Bloomfire EMBEDDED
Product Overview · Confidential
Knowledge in the flow of work

Your agents shouldn't have to leave the screen to find the answer.

Bloomfire Embedded brings your entire knowledge base — and AI-powered answers — directly inside the portals, CRMs, and tools your teams already work in. Zero tab switching. Zero re-training.

The Problem

Agents interrupt live calls or active cases to search a separate knowledge base tab, ask a colleague, or dig through shared drives. Every context switch costs time, breaks focus, and increases the chance of an inconsistent answer. Knowledge exists — it's just not where agents need it.

The Solution

Bloomfire Embedded is a lightweight web component that drops into any portal or app with three lines of code. Agents search and get AI-synthesized answers without leaving the screen they're already on — surfacing the right content from your Bloomfire community in real time.

Key capabilities

Semantic Search

Relevance-ranked results from your Bloomfire community. Articles, series, and Q&A surfaced instantly — with author and date context.

Synapse AI

Conversational AI that synthesizes answers from your knowledge base. Agents ask in plain language and get a direct, sourced response — not just a list of links.

White-Label Ready

Upload your logo, set a brand color, and the widget re-skins instantly. It looks like part of your product — not a third-party tool bolted on.

Where it fits
Claims & Servicing Portals

Adjusters and agents get policy guidance, SOP steps, and state-specific compliance details without leaving the claim they're actively working.

CRM / Salesforce

Sales reps surface competitive battlecards, product specs, and pricing guides mid-opportunity — without breaking focus on the deal.

Customer Help Centers

Customers get AI-powered answers from your knowledge base embedded directly in your support portal — reducing ticket volume and wait times.

Business benefit

Measurable time savings per agent

Each knowledge lookup costs 30–90 seconds in context switching. At 20–40 cases per agent per day, eliminating tab switching reclaims hours per agent per week — with no process change required.

Consistent, compliant answers

Agents under time pressure improvise. Embedded surfaces the authoritative, governance-approved answer at the moment it's needed — reducing compliance risk and inconsistent customer outcomes.

Higher ROI on knowledge investment

Organizations invest heavily in Bloomfire communities. Embedded closes the last-mile gap — ensuring that investment is used at the exact moment a decision is being made, not after the call.

Defensible, sticky integration

Once the widget is embedded in a claims portal or CRM, replacing it requires engineering effort on the customer side — not just a software switch. Embedded deepens the Bloomfire relationship at the operational layer.

Integration

Three lines of code.

No npm, no build step, no framework lock-in. The widget is a standard web component that works in Angular, React, Vue, or plain HTML.

1
Load the script — one tag, once per page
2
Place the element — drop it anywhere in your layout
3
Pass your token — scopes content to your community, silently
<!-- 1. Load once -->
<script src="bloomfire-search.js"></script>

<!-- 2. Place anywhere -->
<bloomfire-search
  community-id="YOUR_ID"
  token="YOUR_TOKEN"
></bloomfire-search>
